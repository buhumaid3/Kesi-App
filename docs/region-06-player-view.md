## Case 51: Balloon Away
*Thursday 5 February · folder tab "ENKARE" · about 28 min*

### OB entry

> A balloon safari company reports its ground-crew chief, Naserian Nkoitoi (31), found dead under the folded balloon envelope at the morning landing site on the plains beside Enkare Conservancy at 07:35. Recorded as a suspected landing accident.

### Site map

- The launch site ↔ Sunrise Balloon Safaris camp: 20 min by 4x4
- The landing site ↔ Sunrise Balloon Safaris camp: 45 min by 4x4
- The launch site ↔ Lodge store, Talek: 60 min by 4x4
- Sunrise Balloon Safaris camp ↔ Lodge store, Talek: 50 min by road

### People
- **Naserian Nkoitoi**, 31, Ground-crew chief, Sunrise Balloon Safaris (victim). The company's first woman crew chief. Counts every propane cylinder after every launch, with a pencil and a tick.
- **Musyoki Mutunga**, 44, Chase-vehicle 2 driver and crewman (suspect). Eleven years with the company. Drives the vehicle that tows the fan trailer.
- **Graham Fenwick**, 61, Balloon pilot (suspect). Born in Nanyuki. Thirty years flying balloons over the Mara. Argued with Naserian about the wind.
- **Simon Saitabao**, 27, Chase-vehicle 1 driver (suspect). Drives the breakfast vehicle. New to the job and anxious to keep it.
- **Zablon Onkware**, 50, Storekeeper, a Talek lodge (suspect). Keeps the store of a lodge in Talek town: flour, fuel, gas.
- **Nkirote Mwiti**, 35, Base radio operator (witness). Runs the base radio at the company camp and logs every call.
- **Kerubo Nyamweya**, 29, Lodge kitchen supervisor, Talek (witness, statement only). Opens the lodge kitchen with the storekeeper every morning.
