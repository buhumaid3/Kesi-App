  1. The room is the frame. No navigation bar, no app chrome. The desk fills the screen; the lamp's pool sits top right and drifts towards whatever you're holding. The status bar reads over the dark desk, so it never needs a band.
  2. The folder is the screen. A manila folder lifts off the stack and opens with a hinge (Reduce Motion: cross-fade). Its region tab (NGARA, case 1's folderTab) is concrete grey with a sodium-amber edge: region 1's colours. The round received-stamp is decorative, never information.
  3. The title is street, the record is institution. "THE LAST FARE" is Big Shoulders at 44 pt, the only display type on the page. Everything you read to solve the case is Courier Prime on paper, at least 15 pt, scaling with Dynamic Type.
  4. The OB entry types itself in. The ledger strip is the OB book's own ruling (green rules, red margin, the time in the margin column). The text arrives at typewriter speed with a synthesized key sound; a tap completes it at once. Amani's highlighter marks the first fact the notebook logs (f_ob_argument), so the player sees, on screen one, that reading carefully is what the game is about.
  5. The photo is evidence, not decoration. The scene photo arrives clipped to the folder, tilted by a seeded angle (always the same for this card), with a print border, grain, slight desaturation and a handwritten label in Amani's hand. The same treatment wraps every generated image in the game, which is what hides drift between generators.
  6. One action, written as an action. The only call to action is a typed index card, "VISIT: NGARA STAGE", the first unlocked location. At least 44 pt tall, full width, with the stamp-red arrow as the only colour.
  7. Navigation is objects with words. Notebook, corkboard, phone (with an unread count) and the carbon-copy charge sheet sit on the desk edge, each labelled in text, each a 70 × 86 pt target. VoiceOver reads them as buttons with their labels; the phone badge is read as "2 new messages".
  8. Colour never carries meaning alone. Stamp red appears three times here (received stamp, arrow, phone badge) and each has a word or a shape. Accepted and returned charge sheets differ by stamp shape and word as well as green and red.
