### SPOILER: solution
**Answer:** who = Peter Kamau. Act window: 22:10–22:40.
s1. The spanner is marked 9N-K, like every tool in Peter's roll, and his roll has a spanner-shaped gap. The weapon came from Peter's tools.
s2. The man who walked away from Moses's matatu at 22:20 wore a driver's maroon jacket, and the only other driver at the stage then was Peter Kamau.

**Eliminations**
- Brian Otieno: Brian was in the Kencom queue from 21:50 to 22:45, heard by the Kencom marshal the whole time.
- Kassim Juma: Kassim was at Mercy's cart buying a smokie when the shout came.
- Mercy Chebet: Mercy was at her own cart serving Kassim when the shout came.

**Verdict reconstruction**
- Friday afternoon. Moses tells Peter he's three contributions behind and that on Monday he'll take the chama book to the sacco chairman. Peter could lose his route.
- 22:00. Peter parks KCZ 902M in the bay next to Moses. At ten, Moses buys his smokie and laughs with Mercy.
- About 22:20. Only two matatus are parked. Peter takes the spanner from his tool roll, follows Moses behind KDK 418T and strikes once.
- He walks back to his own matatu in his maroon jacket, drops the spanner in the gutter, and at 22:31 drives off on his last trip as if nothing happened.
- The fare pouch stays on Moses's belt. It was never about the money in it.

### Glossary
**makanga**: Matatu conductor (Sheng). · **matatu**: Privately run minibus that carries most of Nairobi's commuters. · **sacco**: Savings and credit cooperative; matatus run under a sacco on a fixed route. · **chama**: Savings group whose members pay in regularly and borrow from the pot. · **smokie**: A smoked sausage sold from street carts. · **pasua**: Split open. A smokie pasua is split and filled with kachumbari. · **kachumbari**: Fresh tomato and onion relish. · **kofia**: Embroidered cap, often worn by Muslim men. · **mzee**: Respectful word for an older man. · **mama**: Respectful way to address or refer to an older woman. · **wueh**: Exclamation of shock or dismay. · **githurai**: A Nairobi suburb; 'walk to Githurai' means get stolen and resold. · **bob**: Slang for shillings.
