## Case 21: Kanga za Pwani
*Friday 8 August · folder tab "BIASHARA ST" · about 14 min*

### OB entry
> **OB 17/08/08** · Friday 8 August · 13:41  
> Abubakar Hassan, a shopkeeper on Biashara Street, reports that his neighbour Mwanajuma Athman (58), owner of Kanga za Pwani, was found dead in the back store of her shop at 13:35, during Friday prayers.

### People
- **Mwanajuma Athman**, 58, Owner, Kanga za Pwani (victim). Widow. Thirty-one years selling kanga on Biashara Street. Does her own books every Friday while the street is at prayer.
- **Dancan Odhiambo**, 34, Shop manager, Kanga za Pwani (suspect). Has managed the shop for six years. Runs it alone on busy Saturdays.
- **Faiz Athman**, 27, Mwanajuma's nephew (suspect). Wants to run the shop now. Shouted at her on Tuesday about his late uncle's share.
- **Rukia Hamisi**, 33, Tenant, flat above the shop (suspect). Two months behind on the rent for the flat upstairs.
- **Abubakar Hassan**, 66, Shopkeeper next door (witness). Has sold kikoi next door for forty years. Found her.
- **Pili Juma**, 41, Regular customer (witness). Buys kanga for her shop in Ukunda every month.
- **Ustadh Salim Jeizan**, 59, Mosque committee member (witness, statement only). Keeps the committee book at the mosque on the next street.
- **Kassim Wanje**, 48, Kanga supplier, Mwembe Tayari (witness, statement only). Loads bales onto the Malindi buses.

### Location: Kanga za Pwani, Biashara Street
Friday, half past one. Biashara Street is nearly empty: shutters pulled half down for Jumaa, kangas still hanging in the doorways. Inside Kanga za Pwani, a front counter, shelves of folded cloth, and a back store stacked with bales.

- **Back store**: Mwanajuma lies between the bales. The shop's long brass-tipped measuring rod is on the floor beside her. → *The body in the back store*
- **Counter**: Two glasses of spiced tea on a tray. One is empty. One has barely been touched. → *Two glasses of tea*
- **Her drawer**: Her accounts drawer, open. Papers, a will, a wage book. One envelope is empty. → *Empty statement envelope*
- **Under the envelope**: A copy of her will, folded in three. → *Mwanajuma's will (copy)*
- **Wage book**: A small hardback wage book with a pencil tied to it. → *Wage book*
- **Stock book on the counter**: The stock book, open at last month. Something is clipped behind the page. → *Stock book*
- **Under the counter**: A piece of cardboard pushed out of sight, writing on one side. → *Cardboard sign*
- **Nail by the stairs**: A rent card for the flat upstairs, hanging on a nail. → *Rent card, upstairs flat*
- **Next door**: Abubakar Hassan stands in his own doorway in his prayer clothes, holding the door frame. → *Statement: Abubakar Hassan*

## Case 22: The 06:40 Ferry
*Thursday 14 August · folder tab "LIKONI" · about 14 min*

### OB entry
> **OB 08/14/08** · Thursday 14 August · 07:48  
> Zaituni Omar, a cleaner, reports that fish trader Agnes Auma (44) was found dead in the back room of the Samaki Bora cold store, on the Likoni side of the ferry crossing, at 07:40.

### People
- **Agnes Auma**, 44, Fish trader, Samaki Bora collective (victim). Sells kingfish and snapper on the island. Crosses on the ferry every morning with her crates.
- **Mwanasiti Mwinyi**, 31, Fish trader, same collective (suspect). Bay 7. Says she crosses on the 06:40 ferry every morning, like clockwork.
- **Thomas Owino**, 47, Agnes's ex-husband; hotel security guard (suspect). They have fought for a year over their children's school fees.
- **Mariamu Mbaraka**, 39, Fish trader, same collective (suspect). Signs the collection book "M.M.", like one other trader.
- **Omari Mwinyi**, 36, Mwanasiti's husband; port porter (witness). Works at the old port from six.
- **Zaituni Omar**, 50, Cold-store cleaner (witness). Cleans the cold store and fetches ice. Found Agnes.
- **John Charo**, 25, Boda rider at the ferry (witness, statement only). Waits for fares outside the cold stores.

### Location: Samaki Bora cold store, Likoni
Dawn at the Likoni crossing: thousands of commuters, handcarts and fish crates pouring onto the ferries. Samaki Bora is a block of insulated rooms run by a fish traders' collective. Ice blocks, plastic crates, a collection book on a chain, a bay board on the wall.

- **Back room**: Agnes lies by the ice chest. An ice mallet is on the wet floor. Her apron pocket is zipped and full. → *The body in the back room*
- **Apron pocket**: Her phone, in a plastic bag against the wet. Her sister is on her way with the code. → *Agnes's phone (unlocked by her sister)*
- **Collection book**: Every trader signs out their crates here, with the time. → *Collection book*
- **Bay board**: The allocation sheet: who keeps their fish in which bay. → *Bay allocation sheet*
- **Collective's cash book**: The collective's chama book, on a shelf beside the ledger. → *Collective's chama book*
- **Ferry board outside**: The ferry timetable board at the ramp, painted over many times. → *Ferry timetable board*
- **Cleaner at the door**: Zaituni Omar sits on an upturned crate with a bag of melting ice at her feet. → *Statement: Zaituni Omar*

### Evidence and facts

## Case 23: Turmeric

### OB entry

### People
- **Farida Karimjee**, 64, Owner, Karimjee & Sons Spices (victim). Widow. Runs the spice shop her husband's grandfather opened, in a coral-stone building she will not sell.
- **Yasmin Karimjee**, 55, Farida's sister-in-law; co-owner of the building (suspect). Her late brother's share of the building came to her through the family trust. Short of money. Wants to sell.

- **Drying rack**: A long white dupatta hangs on the rack, washed today. Not quite white. → *Yasmin's white dupatta*
