## Case 11: Number Seven
*Monday 26 May · folder tab "KAYOLE" · about 10 min*

### OB entry
> **OB 27/26/05** · Monday 26 May · 21:04  
> Teresia Ndunge, a roast-maize seller, reports that a boda-boda rider, Erick Odongo (32), vest No. 7, was found face down in the county drainage trench beside the Kayole Spine Road stage at 20:52. His motorbike is still parked at the stage with the key in it.

### People
- **Erick Odongo**, 32, Boda-boda rider, vest No. 7 (victim). Rides from the Kayole Spine Road stage. Bought his motorbike on a loan his ex-girlfriend co-signed, then stopped paying.
- **Sharon Nanjala**, 26, Runs a phone-charging and mandazi kiosk (suspect). Erick's ex-girlfriend. Co-signed the loan on his motorbike. When he stopped paying, the lender started taking her things.
- **Jackson Mutuku**, 47, Stage chairman (suspect). Runs the stage: fees, the rider roster, the fines. Fined Erick last week for unpaid stage fees.
- **Abednego Kerich**, 30, Boda-boda rider, vest No. 12 (suspect). Rival rider. He and Erick came to blows over a customer on Sunday.
- **Teresia Ndunge**, 51, Roast-maize seller (witness). Has roasted maize on the same corner of the stage for nine years. Found Erick when she went to tip out her ash.
- **Peninah Chelimo**, 23, Pump attendant, Petro Mashariki, Fedha (witness, statement only). Works the night pumps at a fictional petrol station in Fedha, about 25 minutes from the Kayole stage by boda at night.

### Location: Kayole Spine Road boda-boda stage
Monday night. A row of motorbikes under a sodium light, riders in numbered vests, a roast-maize brazier and a padlocked phone-charging kiosk. Along the road the county is digging a drainage trench. The stage ground is red murram. The trench floor is grey clay.

- **In the trench**: Erick lies face down in the bottom of the trench, in wet grey clay. One side of his head is badly hurt. His phone is in his jacket pocket. → *The body in the trench*
- **County signboard**: A county board on two poles at the edge of the trench, freshly painted. → *County signboard*
- **Trench floor**: Beside the body, in the soft clay: small boot prints, going in and coming out. → *Boot prints in the trench*
- **Stage shelter**: The riders' shelter. On a nail hangs the stage book, where the chairman writes fees, fines and complaints. → *Stage book*
- **Maize brazier**: Teresia Ndunge stands by her brazier with her arms folded tight. The coals are almost out. → *Statement: Teresia Ndunge*
- **Erick's motorbike**: A red motorbike, key still in the ignition, a loan-company sticker on the mudguard: "Financed by Pikipiki Credit. Do not sell."
- **Charging kiosk**: A small kiosk hung with charging cables and a sign: "CHARGING 20/- · MANDAZI 10/-". The shutter is down and padlocked.

### Location: Sharon's kiosk (opened for Amani)
Sharon unlocks the shutter herself. Inside: a power strip with twelve sockets, a basket of mandazi under a cloth, a charcoal jiko, a stool, and an exercise book on the counter. A gap on the floor where a fridge used to stand.

- **Exercise book on the counter**: Her charge book: every phone she charges, the time and the 20 bob, in her own handwriting. → *Sharon's charge book*
- **Under the stool**: A pair of yellow gumboots, pushed out of sight under the stool. → *Yellow gumboots*
- **By the jiko**: The iron poker for the charcoal jiko, leaning against the wall. Cleaner than anything else in the kiosk. → *Jiko poker*
- **The empty corner**: A clean rectangle on the dusty floor and an unplugged cable. Something heavy stood here until very recently.

### Location: Chief's office, Kayole
A low government building with a noticeboard. Tonight's baraza on stage fees has just ended; plastic chairs are being stacked.

- **Attendance sheet**: The chief's clerk shows the baraza attendance sheet and the minutes she typed during the meeting. → *Baraza attendance sheet*

### Evidence and facts
- **The body in the trench** (physical)
  - Erick was struck on the side of the head and fell or was pushed into the trench. He lies in grey clay.
  - In Erick's pocket: his phone, fully charged, and a paper tag from Sharon's charging kiosk: 'No. 7 — 20/-'.
- **County signboard** (physical)
  - County signboard: 'Grey clay subsoil exposed.' The stage and road are red murram. Grey clay is only inside the trench.
- **Boot prints in the trench** (physical)
  - Small gumboot prints, chevron tread, about size 37, go down into the trench beside the body and climb back out.
- **Stage book** (physical)
  - Stage book, Sunday: 'No. 7 E. Odongo and No. 12 A. Kerich fought over a customer. Both warned.'
  - Stage book, Tuesday: 'No. 7 E. Odongo: stage fees KSh 1,000 unpaid. Fined. — J. Mutuku, chairman.'
- **Statement: Teresia Ndunge** (witness)
  - Teresia sold Erick a maize cob at 20:20. He walked off eating it, towards the trench.
  - At about 20:35 Teresia heard a shout and a thud from the trench. She thought it was a drunk.
  - Teresia stayed at her brazier all evening. Two regulars ate maize beside her from 20:15 to 20:45. Her open sandals carry red murram dust, no clay.
- **Sharon's charge book** (physical)
  - Sharon's charge book, in her handwriting: phones charged at 19:40, 20:10 and 20:25.
- **Yellow gumboots** (physical)
  - Yellow gumboots in her kiosk: size 37, chevron tread, grey clay up to the ankle.
- **Jiko poker** (physical)
  - The kiosk's iron jiko poker has been wiped, but there is a smear of grey clay on the handle.
- **Baraza attendance sheet** (physical)
  - Chief's baraza sheet: J. Mutuku signed in at 18:55. The minutes record him speaking at 20:30. The meeting closed at 21:00.
- **Fuel receipt (Abednego)** (physical)
  - Fuel receipt from Petro Mashariki, Fedha, printed at the pump: 20:28, the plate of Abednego's motorbike, paid in coins.
- **Phone statement: Peninah Chelimo** (witness)
  - Peninah: Abednego rode in about 20:15, queued, paid at 20:28 in coins, argued about change, and left about 20:40 with a passenger towards Utawala, away from Kayole.

### Interviews
**Sharon Nanjala** (cooperative)
> Sharon keeps her hands in her apron pocket. "I heard. Everybody heard. Ask me what you want."
- Says: Sharon: "Erick owed me money. Everyone at this stage knows that."
- Says: Sharon: "I closed the kiosk at half past seven and went home to Kayole Junction."
- *Asked about Erick Odongo:* "Two years we were together. Then he wanted a bike, and the bank wanted a name that pays. Mine."
- *Asked about Jackson: the loan company's men took Sharon's fridge this morning, because Erick stopped paying the bike loan she co-signed.:* "Seventy thousand on a bike I never rode. This morning they carried my fridge out in front of the whole stage. You want me to cry? I'm finished crying."
- *Asked about Yellow gumboots:* "Everyone at this stage wears gumboots when it rains. Look at the road."
- *Asked about Jiko poker:* "It's a poker. I poke the jiko with it."

**Jackson Mutuku** (cooperative)
> Jackson arrives straight from the baraza, still holding his copy of the fee schedule. "One of mine. Number Seven. What happened?"
- Says: Jackson: "I was at the chief's baraza on stage fees all evening."
- *Asked about Erick Odongo:* "He owed the stage a thousand. I fined him, yes. That is my job. You do not kill a man over a thousand bob."
- *Asked about Sharon Nanjala:* "That boy ruined her. She signed for his bike, he stopped paying. The loan people came this morning and took her fridge."
- *Asked about Abednego Kerich:* "Twelve and Seven fought on Sunday over a customer. Boys. I warned them both."

**Abednego Kerich** (guarded; talks once shown: The body in the trench or Statement: Teresia Ndunge)
> Abednego sits on his bike with the engine off and won't look at the trench. "Number Seven? I don't know anything about Number Seven."
- Says: Abednego: "I took a customer to Fedha at eight."
- *Asked about Fuel receipt (Abednego):* "Call the girl at the pump. She remembers me. I paid in coins and she was angry."
- *Asked about Stage book, Sunday: 'No. 7 E. Odongo and No. 12 A. Kerich fought over a customer. Both warned.':* "He pushed me first. Over a customer. On Sunday. That doesn't mean I'd put him in a hole."

**Teresia Ndunge** (cooperative)
> Teresia pours water on the last coals. "Nine years on this corner. Nobody ever died in front of me before."
- *Asked about Erick Odongo:* "He bought maize from me every night. Tonight too, twenty past eight. He walked off eating it, towards the trench, to relieve himself like they all do."
- *Asked about Sharon Nanjala:* "Sharon? They were together, before. Her shutter was down when I found him. I didn't see her close it; I was busy with customers."

### Charge sheet
`{who} killed Erick Odongo at the Kayole stage.`
- **{who}** word bank: Sharon Nanjala · Jackson Mutuku · Abednego Kerich · Teresia Ndunge · Peninah Chelimo

### Hint calls (Mwalimu)
- Tier 1: "She says she went home at half seven. She runs a charging kiosk. Charging kiosks keep books."
- Tier 2: "Read the county sign again: where does grey clay come from? Then look at whose boots are carrying it."

### Glossary
**boda**: Short for boda-boda: a motorbike taxi. · **mandazi**: Fried dough, a little sweet. Sold everywhere with tea. · **baraza**: Public meeting called by the chief, usually in the chief's yard. · **jiko**: Charcoal stove. · **mahindi choma**: Roast maize, cooked over coals at the roadside. · **bob**: Slang for shillings.

## Case 12: Rent Day
*Thursday 5 June · folder tab "JERICHO" · about 11 min*

### OB entry
> **OB 14/05/06** · Thursday 5 June · 18:31  
> Bonface Mutinda, watchman at Baraka Flats, Jericho, reports that the caretaker, Margaret Wangila (45), was found dead in the ground-floor rent office at 18:25 by tenants coming to pay rent. The office rent book is missing.

### People
- **Margaret Wangila**, 45, Caretaker, Baraka Flats (victim). Has run Baraka Flats for eleven years: rent, repairs, water days, quarrels. Writes everything in one blue rent book.
- **Mwita Chacha**, 64, Retired railway clerk; tenant, flat 4C (suspect). Nineteen years in flat 4C. Three months behind on rent since his pension was delayed. An auctioneer's notice is taped to his door.
- **Lydia Anyango**, 28, Tenant, flat 1B; cashier (suspect). Screamed at Margaret on Monday over the water rationing. Paid her rent this afternoon.
- **Wilson Kiprono**, 39, Plumber (suspect). Fixing the building's water tank this week. Margaret refused to pay his last invoice until the leak stopped.
- **Bonface Mutinda**, 58, Night watchman (witness). Sits on a stool at the only gate from 17:00, with the gate book on his knees.
- **Janeth Kwamboka**, 34, Salon owner, Jericho market (witness, statement only). Braids hair at a busy salon in Jericho market.
- **Rehema Wanjiku**, 50, Vegetable seller at the gate (witness, statement only). Sells sukuma and tomatoes from a table beside the Baraka Flats gate every evening.

### Location: Baraka Flats, Jericho
A five-storey walk-up around a concrete courtyard, washing on every balcony. One gate, one watchman, one stairwell. Ground floor, by the stairs: the rent office, its door open, the light on. It is the 5th: rent day.

- **Rent office**: Margaret lies beside her desk. The heavy metal date stamp is on the floor. On the shelf behind her, the slot where the rent book always stands is empty. → *The body and the office*
- **Desk drawer**: The drawer is closed. Inside, a brown envelope marked in Margaret's hand. → *Rent-cash envelope*
- **Gate and stool**: The watchman's stool and the gate book: every visitor in and out, with times. → *Gate book*
- **Vegetable table at the gate**: Rehema Wanjiku is packing her sukuma into a sack, very slowly, so she can keep watching the office. → *Statement: Rehema Wanjiku*
- **Fourth-floor landing**: Flat 4C. A typed notice is taped to the door, its corners curling. → *Notice on the door of 4C*
- **Water tank on the roof**: A new pipe joint on the roof tank, still wrapped in tape. Somebody worked on it today.

### Location: Flat 4C (search)
Two rooms. A railway calendar from 1998, a framed long-service certificate, a single bed, a radio. Everything is very clean. The mattress sits a little high on one side.

- **Under the mattress**: Wrapped in a kanga, flat under the mattress: a blue hard-cover book. → *The missing rent book*
- **Long-service certificate**: "Mwita Chacha. 30 years' loyal service." The frame has been dusted today.

### Evidence and facts
- **The body and the office** (physical)
  - Margaret was struck with the office's heavy metal date stamp.
  - The blue rent book, which never leaves the office shelf, is missing.
- **Rent-cash envelope** (physical)
  - The day's rent cash, KSh 64,000 in an envelope in the drawer, is untouched. Whoever did this came for the book, not the money.
- **Gate book** (physical)
  - Gate book: '17:41 — Plumber W. Kiprono OUT. Mama Margaret walked him to the gate.' The next entry is at 18:20.
- **Statement: Bonface Mutinda** (witness)
  - Bonface: Margaret walked the plumber out at 17:41 and went back to her office. From 17:41 to 18:20 nobody came through the gate in either direction.
  - Bonface: on the 5th everyone works late. The only tenant home that evening was Mzee Mwita in 4C.
  - Bonface: at 18:10 he saw Mzee Mwita go up the stairs with a big blue book under his jacket.
- **Statement: Rehema Wanjiku** (witness)
  - Rehema: Bonface sat on his stool beside her table from 17:00 until the tenants came at 18:20. Nobody passed the gate between the plumber and the tenants.
  - Rehema: on Monday the girl from 1B, Lydia, shouted at Margaret about the water. "The whole building heard."
- **Notice on the door of 4C** (physical)
  - Notice on the door of 4C: distress for rent, arrears January–March, KSh 27,000. Goods to be taken after seven days.
- **Lydia's rent receipt** (physical)
  - Lydia's receipt: No. 0471, today, 17:20, KSh 9,500, signed by Margaret.
- **Mwita's rent receipt** (physical)
  - Mwita's receipt: No. 0498, dated last Saturday, 'Jan–Mar paid in full, KSh 27,000', signed with a scrawl.
- **The missing rent book** (physical · needs a search warrant)
  - Rent book carbons: Saturday reached only 0462. Today's last receipt is 0471. Carbons 0472–0500 are blank, and receipt 0498 was torn out of the back with no carbon behind it.
  - Rent book ledger, flat 4C, in Margaret's hand: 'Jan, Feb, Mar — UNPAID — FINAL NOTICE.'
- **Phone statement: Janeth Kwamboka** (witness)
  - Janeth: Lydia was in her chair for braids from 17:30 until after 19:00. "Three of us worked on her. She didn't even go to the toilet."

### Interviews
**Mwita Chacha** (cooperative)
> Mwita opens the door of 4C before Amani knocks. He is wearing a tie. "Come in, officer. I know about Margaret. Terrible."
- Says: Mwita: "I paid all my arrears on Saturday. Margaret gave me a receipt."
- Says: Mwita: "Nineteen years I have lived in 4C. I have never been late until this year."
- *Asked about Notice on the door of 4C:* "A mistake. The auctioneer doesn't know I paid. I will take the receipt to his office on Monday."
- *Asked about Margaret Wangila:* "Eleven years she has been caretaker. She was strict, but she was fair. Until this year."
- *Asked about Bonface: at 18:10 he saw Mzee Mwita go up the stairs with a big blue book under his jacket.:* "Bonface is an old man. He sees a man with a book. Many people carry books."

**Lydia Anyango** (cooperative)
> Lydia touches her new braids as if checking they are still there. "I shouted at her on Monday. Everybody heard. Now you think I killed her."
- Says: Lydia: "I paid my rent at twenty past five and went to get my hair done."
- *Asked about Margaret Wangila:* "Four days without water on my floor. Four days. I shouted, yes. Then I paid her today like always."
- *Asked about Lydia's rent receipt:* "There. Twenty past five. Then I went straight to Janeth's in the market."
- *Asked about Janeth Kwamboka:* "Call her. She'll tell you how long braids take."

**Wilson Kiprono** (guarded; talks once shown: Gate book)
> Wilson stands at the gate with his toolbag and won't come in. "I fix pipes. I don't know anything."
- Says: Wilson: "She walked me out herself. She was alive and shouting about my invoice."
- *Asked about Margaret Wangila:* "She owed me for the tank. She said the 10th. I believed her. Now who will pay me?"

**Bonface Mutinda** (cooperative)
> Bonface stands up from his stool for the first time all evening. "I sat here. I watched this gate. Nobody passed."
- *Asked about Mwita Chacha:* "Mzee Mwita? Nineteen years here. Retired from the railway. A gentleman. At ten past six he went up with that blue book under his jacket. I thought she lent it to him."
- *Asked about Wilson Kiprono:* "Mama Margaret walked him out herself at 17:41. She was complaining about his invoice the whole way."

### Charge sheet
`{who} killed Margaret Wangila at Baraka Flats.`
- **{who}** word bank: Mwita Chacha · Lydia Anyango · Wilson Kiprono · Bonface Mutinda · Janeth Kwamboka

### Hint calls (Mwalimu)
- Tier 1: "The watchman sat at the only gate. Ask him who came in, who went out, and who was already inside."
- Tier 2: "A receipt has a number. The book has carbons. On Saturday, how far had the numbers got?"

### Glossary
**mzee**: Respectful word for an older man. · **mama**: Mother; also a respectful way to address or name a woman. · **madalali**: Agents or brokers; here, the auctioneer's men who come to take goods. · **kanga**: Printed cotton cloth, often with a proverb along the edge. · **sukuma**: Sukuma wiki: collard greens, the everyday vegetable.

## Case 13: Barwaqo Mall
*Wednesday 11 June · folder tab "EASTLEIGH" · about 12 min*

### OB entry
> **OB 33/11/06** · Wednesday 11 June · 19:12  
> Fatuma Ali, a tea seller, reports that Hodan Farah (34), fabric wholesaler, stall 2-14, Barwaqo Mall, First Avenue, Eastleigh, was found dead in her stall's storage cubicle at 19:05, shortly after the mall closed.

### People
- **Hodan Farah**, 34, Wholesaler of printed fabric, stall 2-14 (victim). Sells printed cotton by the bale to retailers from all over Kenya. Gives credit to buyers she trusts.
- **Josephine Mueni**, 52, Clothing retailer from Machakos (suspect). Buys fabric on credit from Hodan and pays by bank deposit slip. Has done for five years.
- **Abdirahman Mohamed**, 44, Hodan's cousin and co-owner of the stall (suspect). He and Hodan are in a dispute over her late father's share of the business. The elders are mediating.
- **Rajesh Patel**, 58, Fabric importer and supplier (suspect). Supplies Hodan from his godown in Industrial Area. They argued last month over his new prices.
- **Fatuma Ali**, 49, Tea seller (witness). Walks the four floors of Barwaqo Mall all day with two flasks of shaah. Found Hodan.
- **Lameck Onyancha**, 33, Handcart porter (witness). Pushes a mkokoteni in the lanes behind First Avenue. Carries bales to the matatu stages.
- **Sheikh Ibrahim Hassan**, 66, Imam, mosque on 6th Street (witness, statement only). Leads prayers at a mosque two streets from the mall.

### Location: Barwaqo Mall, First Avenue, Eastleigh
Four floors of fabric and clothing stalls. Closing time is 19:00 and the shutters are coming down. Stall 2-14 on the second floor is stacked with bales of printed cotton. A storage cubicle at the back has a plywood door. On the ground floor, a bank branch, its hours painted on the glass.

- **Storage cubicle**: Hodan lies between the bales. A steel bale hook is on the floor beside her. A glass of tea stands cold outside the door. → *The body in the cubicle*
- **Invoice book on the counter**: Hodan's carbon invoice book, open at today's page. → *Invoice book*
- **Stapled to the invoice**: A bank deposit slip stapled to the last invoice. Something is written on it in pencil. → *Bank deposit slip*

- **Bank glass, ground floor**: The bank branch at the mall entrance. Its opening hours are painted on the glass door. → *Bank hours on the glass*
- **Tea seller**: Fatuma Ali sits on an upturned crate with her two flasks, shaking. → *Statement: Fatuma Ali*
- **Porters' lane outside**: Behind the mall, the porters' lane. One porter, Lameck Onyancha, is counting coins and watching the police. → *Statement: Lameck Onyancha*

- **Invoice book** (physical)

## Case 14: Full Time
*Sunday 15 June · folder tab "UMOJA" · about 12 min*

### OB entry
> **OB 08/15/06** · Sunday 15 June · 21:41  
> Monicah Njeri, a waitress, reports that the owner of Goal Pub & Screen, Umoja Inner Core, Josiah Mutai (56), was found dead in the storeroom at 21:30, at the end of the Sunday match. About sixty customers were in the pub.

### People
- **Josiah Mutai**, 56, Owner, Goal Pub & Screen (victim). Built the pub from a kiosk with one television. Tight with money, loud on match nights.
- **Ian Kamotho**, 27, Screen operator and DJ ("DJ Kamo") (suspect). Has run the projector booth for two years. Fired this afternoon; Josiah refused to pay him two months' wages.
- **Tobias Okoth**, 24, Regular customer; mechanic (suspect). Shouted at Josiah at half-time over a KSh 300 tab. Says he never left his seat.
- **Purity Chepngetich**, 49, Josiah's wife (suspect). Inherits the pub. She wanted to sell it; he refused.
