Contact

Email kesicasefiles@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, and the case number if it's about a case.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a case. Open the phone and call Mwalimu. Hints are free; they only lower that case's stars. Every case can be finished.

No sound. Settings → Test sound plays a cue, music and a voice and tells you what might be muting it. Check "Play sound when the phone is on silent".

Text too small, or hard to read. Settings → Text size, and Plain text mode (the system font everywhere).

Too much movement. Settings → Motion, or turn on Reduce Motion in iOS Settings → Accessibility.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on the device after every action and is kept through updates. Deleting the app deletes it.
