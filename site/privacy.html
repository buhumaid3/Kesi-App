Privacy policy

Kesi: Kenya Case Files ("Kesi", "the app") does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

What the app keeps

Your progress (cases closed, stars, notes on the corkboard, settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it at any time in Settings → Erase all progress.

What the app does not do

No accounts or sign-in. No analytics or tracking. No advertising. No third-party services that collect data. The app makes no network requests while you play.

Device features

If you turn on "Tilt to look around", the app reads the phone's motion sensor to move the photos on screen. The readings are used on the device at that moment and are not stored or sent anywhere. Sound and vibration settings are stored with your progress.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, optional cloud sync), this page will say so before the app does, with the new date at the top.
